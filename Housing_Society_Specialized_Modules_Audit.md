## 1. Member Reports Suite (Excluding Outstanding & Member Ledgers)

This suite encompasses formal billing, collection, adjustment documents, reconciliation statements, and voucher registers.

### 1.1 Member Bill Format (`bill-format`)
- **Codebase Path:** `modules/member-reports/bill-format/` | `Backend/Services/MemberReportService.cs` (`GetBillFormat`)
- **Implemented Architecture & Formulas:**
  - Queries `jeevika_erp.SocMemberBill` joined with `SocMember`, `SocMemberBillItem`, and `SocBillType`.
  - Supports dual formats: Standard Bill of Supply and GST Tax Invoice (SAC Code `999598`).
  - Breakdown: Line items from `SocMemberBillItem`, CGST (9%), SGST (9%), prior arrears, delayed interest, and net payable.
  - Presentation includes: Member Code, Flat No, Wing, Floor, Carpet Area (Sq.Ft.), Billing Period, Due Date, Bank Details (A/C No, IFSC), Dynamic UPI QR Code, and Signatory Attestation Blocks.
- **Housing Society Statutory Compliance:**
  - **What is CORRECT:**
    - Bifurcates current billing demand from historical arrears and interest.
    - Accurately generates number-to-words INR translation (`NumberToWordsINR`).
    - Accommodates co-owners (`MemName2`), meeting Model Bye-Law 24 requirements.
  - **Gaps & Discrepancies:**
    - *Missing Statutory Due Date & Penalty Clause:* Model Bye-Law 68 requires a mandatory standard clause: *"Payment received after [Due Date] shall incur simple interest @ 21% p.a. from the due date until settlement."* Currently missing from static print footer.
    - *Payment Slip Counterfoil:* Model Bye-Law formats provide a detachable perforated "Bank/Office Counterfoil" at the bottom of physical bills. Currently, the bill prints only as a single consolidated invoice without a detachable receipt counterfoil.
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Add customizable statutory footer clauses and an optional perforated counterfoil in `henu-os-design.js` and `bill-format.js`.

### 1.2 Member Receipt Voucher (`receipt`)
- **Codebase Path:** `modules/member-reports/receipt/` | `Backend/Services/MemberReportService.cs` (`GetReceipt`)
- **Implemented Architecture & Formulas:**
  - Retrieves collections from `SocVoucherHeader` matching `VoucherType IN ('Receipt', 'MemberReceipt')`.
  - Captures receipt date, payment instrument (Cash vs Cheque/Digital), cheque number, cheque date, drawer bank name, and narration.
- **Housing Society Statutory Compliance:**
  - **What is CORRECT:**
    - Enforces society metadata disclosure (Registration No, PAN, TAN).
    - Preserves voucher sequence numbers generated by the backend.
  - **Gaps & Discrepancies:**
    - *VoucherType Filter Mismatch:* In `MemberReportService.cs` line 316, the query filters by `VoucherType IN ('Receipt', 'MemberReceipt')`. However, `SocVoucherHeader` records generated by other controllers (e.g. `MemberReceiptController`, `DashboardController`, `BankRecoController`) frequently store voucher types as `'MRV'` or `'RV'`. Receipts saved with type `'MRV'` are silently excluded from `GetReceipt`!
    - *Cheque Realization Disclosure:* Under cooperative accounting, receipts issued against physical cheques are conditional upon clearing. They must bear the statutory watermark/endorsement: *"Subject to realization of cheque"*. Currently missing.
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Expand SQL query condition to: `v.VoucherType IN ('Receipt', 'MemberReceipt', 'MRV', 'RV')` and inject the conditional realization disclaimer for non-cash instruments.

### 1.5 Member Control Account Reconciliation (`member-control-account`)
- **Codebase Path:** `modules/member-reports/member-control-account/` | `MemberReportService.cs` (`GetControlAccount`)
- **Implemented Architecture & Formulas:**
  - Implements the month-by-month reconciliation:
    $$\text{Closing Debtors} = \text{Opening Debtors} + \text{Total Billed} + \text{Debit Notes} - \text{Total Receipts} - \text{Credit Notes}$$
  - Pulls opening debtors directly from `SocMember (OpPrincipal + OpInterest)`.
- **Housing Society Statutory Compliance:**
  - **What is CORRECT:**
    - Provides auditors with an aggregate reconciliation between total member billings and General Ledger debits.
  - **Gaps & Discrepancies:**
    - *Omission of Advance Receipts (`LIA-1020`):* The formula treats all receipts as reductions of debtors. However, if a member pays an advance, that receipt creates an increase in the Advance from Members liability rather than a reduction of debtors. Aggregating all receipts without bifurcating dues reduction vs advance collection distorts net debtor balances.
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Differentiate receipts between dues recovery (crediting `ASS-1025`) and advance intake (crediting `LIA-1020`) in the reconciliation table.

## 3. System Utilities Suite

### 3.1 Financial Year Rollover (`new-year-cf`, `last-year-bf`, `year-extension`)
- **Codebase Path:** `modules/utilities/new-year-cf/`, `last-year-bf/`, `year-extension/` | `Backend/Controllers/UtilityController.cs`
- **Implemented Architecture & Formulas:**
  - Evaluates Closing Financial Year accounts:
    - **Assets (`GrpMainId: 1`) & Liabilities (`GrpMainId: 2`):** Carried forward to the new financial year opening balance (`SocOpeningBalance`).
    - **Income (`GrpMainId: 3`) & Expenses (`GrpMainId: 4`):** Operational opening balance reset strictly to `0.00`.
- **Housing Society Statutory Compliance:**
  - **What is CORRECT:**
    - Properly treats nominal accounts by closing them at fiscal year-end, preserving the rule that Income and Expenses cannot carry forward an opening operational balance.
  - **CRITICAL GAP (Double-Entry Imbalance on Rollover):**
    - In `UtilityController.NewYearCfExecute` (lines 1640–1745), the code calculates `surplusDeficit = totalIncome - totalExpense`. It carries forward Assets and Liabilities, but **does not create an opening balance entry for the Net Surplus/Deficit into Statutory Reserve / General Reserve / Accumulated Surplus (`GrpMainId: 2`)** in the new financial year!
    - **Consequence:** The new financial year's opening Trial Balance will be out of balance by exactly the amount of the surplus or deficit ($\text{Total Debit Opening} \neq \text{Total Credit Opening}$)!
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Update `NewYearCfExecute` to automatically credit Net Surplus (or debit Net Deficit) to the designated General Reserve / Accumulated Surplus account in `SocOpeningBalance` for `NextFYId`.

## 6. Communication Engine

- **Codebase Path:** `modules/communication/` | `Backend/Controllers/CommunicationController.cs`
- **Implemented Architecture:** Over 4,000 lines of backend logic featuring:
  - Meta WhatsApp Cloud API integration with webhook support (`webhook/whatsapp`).
  - SMTP/TLS Email dispatch engine (`email/config`, `test-connection`, `test-send`).
  - Two-way conversation messaging and live inbox.
  - Outbox retry engine and message queuing for members and committee.
  - Automated PDF attachment generation (`Receipt_{flatNo}_{receiptNo}.pdf`).
- **Housing Society Statutory Compliance:**
  - **What is CORRECT:**
    - Generates electronic delivery logs with timestamps and delivery statuses, fulfilling statutory requirements for proof of service.
    - Automated receipt dispatch immediately upon payment clearing.
  - **Gaps & Discrepancies:**
    - *Notice Period Validation:* Model Bye-Laws mandate strict notice horizons before meetings:
      - Annual General Body Meeting (AGM): **14 Clear Days**
      - Special General Body Meeting (SGM): **5 Clear Days**
      - Managing Committee Meeting: **3 Clear Days**
      Currently, the communication queue accepts any delivery date without validating whether the notice complies with statutory lead times.
    - *Broadcast Privacy & Defamation Risks:* Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and cooperative society norms, broadcasting individual flat arrears and defaulter notices in general community WhatsApp groups (`whatsapp-community`) creates severe legal defamation liability for the society. Defaulter notices must be restricted strictly to direct 1-to-1 channels (`whatsapp-member`).
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Add statutory lead-time validation for meeting circulars and enforce strict 1-to-1 routing for debt recovery notices.
